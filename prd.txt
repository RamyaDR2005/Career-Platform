PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)

Project Name

AI Career Intelligence Platform

Version

1.0

Vision

Create an AI-powered platform that helps engineering students become placement-ready by providing personalized career guidance, resume analysis, skill-gap detection, mock interviews, and intelligent job matching while enabling placement officers and recruiters to automate recruitment workflows.

Product Goals
Business Goals
Increase student placement success.
Reduce manual work for placement cells.
Improve recruiter efficiency.
Demonstrate enterprise AI capabilities using IBM watsonx.ai.
User Goals

Students should be able to:

Know where they stand.
Know what to learn.
Improve resumes.
Prepare interviews.
Apply confidently.
