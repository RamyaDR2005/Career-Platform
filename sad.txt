SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE DOCUMENT (SAD)

Project: AI Career Intelligence Platform

Version: 1.0

1. System Overview

The AI Career Intelligence Platform is a cloud-based SaaS application that connects Students, Recruiters, Placement Officers, and Administrators. It leverages IBM watsonx.ai to provide intelligent career guidance, automate placement workflows, and support data-driven decision making.
